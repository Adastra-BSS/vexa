- **A Teams call that drops under the bot ends promptly, as a removal (INATTE-17).** When Teams drops the bot with no ending message on screen, the bot used to sit idle for five minutes and then record the meeting as completed, with the silence reported to everyone invited. Now the bot ends the call after 30 seconds once the hangup button, the roster and the remote audio all show it is gone, and the meeting is reported as evicted with `removal_kind: connection_lost`. Set `BOT_CALL_LOST_WINDOW_MS` to change the window, or to 0 to turn the check off.
