- **Leaving a meeting: one policy, and the meeting is told why (IARD-151).** The bot leaves on its own when the meeting is over or quiet, and says so in the Teams chat when it is a quiet, nobody-joined or active-cap exit: no speech for 40 minutes with people still joined; nobody joined within 15 minutes of admission; everyone left (silent, 5 minutes); the 12-hour active cap. A meeting running past its scheduled end is no longer cut off, and host removal stays `evicted`. See [How to send a bot](/how-to/send-a-bot).
