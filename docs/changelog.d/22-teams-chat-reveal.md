- **The Teams join notice survives a parked chat pane (INATTE-25, #22).** Teams sometimes opens the meeting chat with no Chat button and the pane parked off-screen, so the notice failed as `chat_disabled` after a two-minute wait. With no Chat button for 45 seconds the bot now brings the parked pane back on-screen once and posts the notice through it. `bot.join_notice_posted` reports `compose_via` (`known` or `fallback`) and `pane_opened`, and the new `bot.chat_pane_revealed` event reports whether a pane was moved.
